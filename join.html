Join the Lab

Opportunities for students and postdocs.

Why Join Us?

We provide a supportive and collaborative environment for young scientists to investigate big questions in planet formation. Group members have direct access to cutting-edge data from ALMA and JWST.

We value independence, creativity, and open communication. We strongly support our students in applying for fellowships (e.g., NSF GRFP) and attending conferences.

How to Apply

Please send an email to Prof. Zhang with the following:

  • Your CV / Resume
  • A brief statement of your research interests and why you want to join our group.
  • (For undergrads) Unofficial transcript.
